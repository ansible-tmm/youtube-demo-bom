Privacy Policy

Ansible YouTube BOM · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers the ansible-youtube-bom Google OAuth client used by the youtube-demo-bom project to sync YouTube channel metadata into a Google Sheet.

Who we are

This app is operated for internal Ansible / Red Hat technical marketing use. Contact: scavanau@redhat.com.

What we access

When you authorize the app, it requests:

  • youtube.readonly — read-only access to YouTube channel and video metadata (titles, IDs, publish times, privacy/status, view/like/comment counts, durations) for channels you manage, including Brand Accounts.

The Google Sheet you connect also stores human-entered fields you add yourself (demo slugs, owners, review status, notes). Those are not sent to YouTube.

How we use data

  • To list videos from the configured channel (and optional extra video IDs).
  • To write that metadata into your Google Sheet tabs such as _youtube_raw and related BOM views.
  • To keep a refresh token in Apps Script script properties so scheduled sync can run without weekly re-login.

Data is used only to operate this sync tool for the authorized account.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use YouTube data for advertising.
  • We do not publish private/unlisted video content beyond what you store in your own Sheet.
  • We do not share OAuth tokens with third parties.

Storage

OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are stored in your Google Cloud project / Apps Script project (script properties) and, optionally, a local token.json on the machine used to mint the Brand Account refresh token. Sheet data lives in your Google Spreadsheet.

Retention & deletion

You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account permissions. You can also delete the refresh token from Apps Script script properties and remove rows from the Sheet. After revocation, the app can no longer call the YouTube API on your behalf.

Third parties

The app calls Google APIs only (YouTube Data API v3, Google Sheets / Apps Script). No other analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

Changes

We may update this policy as the project evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
